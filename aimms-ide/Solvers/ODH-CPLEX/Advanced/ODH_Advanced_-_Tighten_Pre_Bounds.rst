Tighten Pre Bounds
==================



:Type:	Selection
:Range:	The settings listed below
:Default:	Off



This option sets the level of bound tightening in the ODH presolved model. Possible values are:



    *	Off
    *	Least aggressive
    *	Moderate
    *	Most aggressive




**Note**

*	This option was added in ODH-CPLEX 8.2.
